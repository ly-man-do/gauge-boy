Gauge Boy TRIM, mockup v2 (night)

Top: a panel per bank (one on inline engines) with two center-zero LED bars, -25 % to +25 %: the top one the long (learned) trim, the bottom one the short, each with its value at the right, lit from 0 in the zone colours, an arrow in the end segment past the range; the bank's label runs up the panel's left edge. Under them RPM, load and the engine state. Bottom: the poll rate, the hint, REC while recording, LIVE's Stop | Record toggle, (B) Back and (Y) Info. Info shows the current TRIM screen's explanation.
