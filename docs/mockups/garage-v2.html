Gauge Boy GARAGE, TRIP and MILEAGE, mockup v2 (night)

Garage shows one car's card (its photo, nickname, car, VIN, odometer in LCD digits, logged distance and bests); half circles go to the other cars. Photo opens the outer camera live on the top screen; Take Photo (or A) takes the shot, then Use Photo, Retake or Remove (back to the mascot car). Trip shows the trip in big LCD digits. The mileage log is a scrolling list like LOG's; an entry opens its page with Edit and Delete, and Delete asks first. (Y) Info holds today's explanations; (X) New Entry sits across from Back. The photos here are drawn stand-ins. Sample values (the VIN is made up).
