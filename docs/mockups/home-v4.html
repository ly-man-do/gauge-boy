Gauge Boy HOME, mockup v4 (night)

New 3DS XL: top screen 4.88 in, 400x240 per eye (800x240 with 3D); bottom screen 4.18 in, 320x240. Both are about 96 pixels per inch, so they are drawn at the same scale, in true proportion. "1x" is roughly actual size on a standard display. Icons and numbers are placeholders. In the app, text is the 3DS system font, loaded from the console at runtime. home-v4.local.html previews it with a Rodin font that is kept out of the repo. The status bar will be restyled to match the 3DS system bar (not shown here).
