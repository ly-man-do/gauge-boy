Gauge Boy TRIM, mockup v1 (night)

Top: a chunky center-zero dial per bank (one on inline engines), -25 % to +25 %, its segments in the zone colors and lit from 0 to the long trim; the thick yellow needle is the long trim, the thin white one the short; the big number is the total, in its zone color. Under them the engine state and a hint (hints, not diagnoses). Bottom: what trims mean and the zones, the poll rate (or REC), the Stop | Record toggle from LIVE, and Back. DIAG keeps the brief's link: with P0171 / P0172 / P0174 / P0175 stored or pending, a Fuel Trims button sits under the codes. Sample values.
