SHIFT LIGHT
rpm
LEDs fill from 60 % of the shift point and flash at it: SHIFT!

The LEDs on the top screen fill as the RPM rises and flash at the shift point.

Set the shift point while parked, a little under the redline (it's saved). − and + change it by 100 rpm.

Older cars send RPM a few times a second; the light fills in between.

Mount the 3DS firmly, screen facing you, then tap Calibrate parked with the engine idling (the 3DS still).

Up on the circle is speeding up, down is braking, sides are cornering. Which way is forward is learned on the first launch (OBD speed).

Calibrate again after moving it. The sensor numbers wobble a little at rest: that's normal and averaged out.
