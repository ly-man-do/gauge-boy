# Changelog

All notable changes to Gauge Boy are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Milestone releases: M0 = 0.0.x, M1 = 0.1.x, ... M7 = 1.0.0.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- **SETUP > WI-FI is simpler**: CHECK & CONNECT goes through every step (Wi-Fi, joining the chosen slot if needed; reaching the adapter; the adapter answering; the car) and connects. When it works it just says "Connected." with the adapter and the car; when it doesn't it says "Failed at:" the step and what to fix, and names the slot's IP and DNS when they differ from the guide. CONNECTION GUIDE opens the setup steps (its CHECK is the same one). The separate Connect and slot-check buttons are gone.
- **GARAGE is reached from HOME's Garage card only** (the GARAGE button left TRACK).
- **No license for now**: the MIT license is removed (owner decision); all rights are reserved until the owner chooses one. Copies published before (up to 0.2.0) stay MIT. About no longer says MIT. Third-party parts keep their own licenses (CREDITS.md).
- **FUN is now TRACK**: the HOME card reads TRACK, "Track modes", for the timers, shift light, G-meter and the other modes coming in version 0.4.
- **GRAPH draws smooth curves**: the line passes smoothly through each reading (a monotone cubic, so it never overshoots and invents no peaks) instead of stepping from one held value to the next, and a faint band behind it shows each moment's lowest and highest value, so short spikes stay visible.
- **The name is now "Gauge Boy"** (two words), full name "Gauge Boy: OBD-II Deluxe", on the HOME menu, the banner, every screen, the logs and the docs. Files and folders follow: the release files are `gauge-boy.cia` and `gauge-boy.3dsx`, and the SD card folder is `sdmc:/3ds/gauge-boy/`. On its first start, Gauge Boy moves the settings, logs, drives and code history from the old `sdmc:/3ds/gaugeboy/` folder (an old `.3dsx` stays there; delete it once the new one runs). The `.cia` keeps its title ID, so it installs over the old one. Drive logs recorded before the change still open in LOG.

### Fixed

- **G-meter calibration works with the sensor's normal jitter**: at rest the 3DS motion sensor's numbers wobble a couple of units (about 0.005 g), and an idling engine shakes the mount a little more. CALIBRATE now averages a second of readings and only fails on real movement (a typical shake over 0.05 g, or one bump over 0.3 g), shows its progress, and says how much it shook if it fails. The sensor numbers on the top screen are smoothed so they hold still, and the session log records each calibration.

### Added

- **Fuel rate and instant economy**: LIVE's Drive view (and All and Custom, whenever speed is polled) shows the fuel rate in gal/h (L/h) and the instant economy in mpg (L/100 km). It uses the car's own fuel rate (PID 5E, or 9D on newer cars like the Ascent); otherwise it is estimated ("Fuel est.") from MAF, or from MAP, RPM and intake air with the engine size set in SETUP (the Ridgeline). REC records both as columns.
- **More live data**: fuel system status (open or closed loop, in Idle & vacuum and Air & fuel), commanded lambda, catalyst temperatures, absolute load, relative and second throttle positions, accelerator pedal positions, commanded throttle, EGR command and error, evap purge and pressure, actual, demanded, reference and friction torque, and the codes-cleared and CEL-on counters. Each car shows the ones it reports.
- **DIAG says when the codes were cleared**: a "Cleared" line with the warm-ups, distance and time since (PIDs 30, 31, 4E), and when the CEL is on, how far it has been driven that way (21, or the time, 4D). A recent clear can hide problems on a used car.
- **Oxygen sensors in LIVE**: a new O2 sensors view shows every oxygen sensor the car reports, with RPM and the short-term trims: narrow band sensors as a voltage (PIDs 14-1B, switching between about 0.1 V lean and 0.9 V rich) and wide band (air-fuel) sensors as lambda (PIDs 24-2B and 34-3B, 1.000 the ideal mixture, below rich, above lean), named by bank and sensor (B1S1 is bank 1, before the catalytic converter). They are in All and Custom too, and REC records them. The 2008 Ridgeline has wide band sensors before the converters and narrow band ones after.
- **Garage on HOME**: the top-left status card is now Garage (a teal car), which opens the garage cards. The CEL lamp moved to the DIAG card: it turns amber with a lit lamp and says "CEL ON" when the check engine light is on.
- **Trip meter** (GARAGE > TRIP): distance from the car's own odometer since RESET TRIP (parked), in big digits, live when it's the connected car and otherwise from the last saved reading. Cars without the odometer PID are pointed to the mileage log. Kept per car in `trip.csv`.
- **Mileage log** (GARAGE > MILEAGE LOG): NEW ENTRY asks on the number pad for the odometer at the start (filled in from the last entry) and the end (from the odometer when known), the fuel used and its price per gallon (US) or litre (metric; optional, filled in from the last entry), and shows each entry's mpg (or L/100 km) and cost, the average economy, the total spent and the cost a mile (or km). The entries are a list on the bottom screen (newest first, 3 a page): tap one to select it, then EDIT asks the same questions filled in with it, or DELETE removes it after a second tap. Kept per car in `mileage.csv` (km and litres).
- **Odometer readings with commas** (29,958 mi) in LIVE, the garage card and the trip meter.
- **Odometer** (PID A6, newer cars such as the Ascent): in LIVE's Drive view (and All and Custom), recorded by REC, and kept per car in `odometer.csv` so the garage card shows the last reading. Also the **transmission gear ratio** (PID A4) where the car reports it.
- **LIVE > Transmission**: on CAN cars, a view of the transmission module's own standard PIDs, asked of that module alone (it reads which ones it supports first). Older cars (K-line, J1850) say it needs a CAN car.
- **DIAG lists the modules that answered** the scan (Engine, Transmission, others by address), and a code's description on the top screen says which module set it ("from Transmission"). Gauge Boy asks once more with headers on at the end of the scan to find out; adapters without headers skip it.
- **ACHIEVEMENTS** (TRACK > ACHIEVEMENTS): 12 stickers that unlock by themselves and pop up as "Unlocked: ...!" on the bottom screen: First Scan, Clean Bill of Health (CEL off, no codes, every monitor ready), Code Breaker (codes cleared), On the Record (a drive recorded), 100 Miles Logged, Full Garage (3 cars), Stopwatch (a Time Attack result), Perfect Sync, Rev Sync Star (rank S), Hypermiler (an Eco trip ranked S), Redline (the shift light flashes) and Pulling G's (0.5 g on the G-meter). The list shows each one's date or how to get it; unlocks are kept in `achievements.csv`. Sticker art comes with M5.
- **GARAGE** (TRACK > GARAGE): a card for each car Gauge Boy knows (the connected one first, then every car in the bests, the saved engine-off MAP readings, the nicknames and recorded drives with a VIN), with < > between them. Each card shows the car as its VIN decodes (or as set in SETUP), its VIN, a nickname (NICKNAME opens the keyboard; saved in `garage.csv`), the distance logged in recorded drives and how many, and its personal bests from TIME ATTACK, REV SYNC and ECO. The distance comes from each drive log's speed over time and is cached per file (`garage-drives.csv`), so only new drives are read. A plain frame stands in for the picture until the camera photo and mascot art come.
- **ECO CHALLENGE** (TRACK > ECO): live fuel economy (mpg or L/100 km, gal/h or L/h when stopped) and the trip's average, distance and fuel, from the MAF or, on cars without one (such as the Ridgeline and Tracker), from MAP, RPM, intake temperature and the engine size. A score for smooth driving: gentle starts and stops, RPM under 2800 and no long idling (the first minute of a stop is free), ranked S 90+, A 80+, B 65+, else C after a minute and 0.5 km of driving, with hard starts and stops counted and a tip for each. The trip runs while the screen is open; RESET TRIP (parked) starts a new one, and BACK keeps the car's best trip score in bests.csv. Fuel figures are estimates.
- **Engine size** in SETUP > VEHICLE (< > steps 0.6 to 8.0 L), saved as `engine` in config.ini's `[vehicle]`, for ECO's fuel estimate. The VIN row moved next to BACK to make room.
- **REV SYNC** (TRACK > REV SYNC): a parked-only mini game. Each of 5 rounds shows a target RPM on a tach bar; at GO, blip the throttle so the peak lands on it. PERFECT within 75 rpm (1000 points), GREAT 200 (600), GOOD 400 (300), OK 700 (100), else MISS; GREAT or better in a row builds a combo (+200 a round). The game ends with a score and a rank from S to D, and each car's high score is kept in bests.csv. It's locked unless the car's speed reads 0, and stops at once if the car moves or speed readings stop. Targets stay between 2000 and 4500 rpm and 1000 under the shift light's shift point.
- **G-METER** (TRACK > G-METER): the car's G-forces from the 3DS's own motion sensor, as a ball on a circle (1 g at the edge, a ring at 0.5 g; up is speeding up, down braking, the sides cornering), with the side, long and total g and their peaks (RESET PEAKS clears them). Mount the 3DS at any angle and tap CALIBRATE parked with it still: Gauge Boy measures gravity to know which way is up and how strong 1 g reads, and ignores bumps. Which way is forward is learned on the first launch (OBD speed from 0 to 10 km/h within 4 s), so the ball points the right way however the 3DS faces. CALIBRATE and RESET PEAKS are refused while moving.
- **SHIFT LIGHT** (TRACK > SHIFT LIGHT): a row of ten LEDs on the top screen, green, amber and red, that fills as the RPM rises (from 60 % of the shift point) and flashes with SHIFT! at the shift point, like an aftermarket shift light, with the RPM in big digits. Set the shift point with -100 / +100 while parked (2000 to 9900 rpm, default 6000); it's saved as `shift_rpm` in a new `[track]` section of config.ini. It polls only RPM (and speed once a second), and between readings it carries the RPM along its trend for up to 0.3 s, so the LEDs rise smoothly on older cars that send RPM a few times a second.
- **TIME ATTACK** (TRACK > TIME ATTACK): 0-60 mph (0-100 km/h), 60-0 (100-0) braking with the stopping distance, and the 1/4 mile with the trap speed and 0-60 time. A one-time closed-course notice first. Pick a run while parked (refused while moving); the timer waits for the car to stand still, counts down 3, 2, 1, GO and starts by itself when the car moves (braking: when you slow under 60 after driving faster), shows the result in big digits (NEW RECORD! when it beats this car's best) and arms itself again once you stop: no touch needed while moving. It reads only vehicle speed, as fast as the adapter allows (no battery voltage reads in between), and estimates the launch and crossing moments between readings; results are labeled as estimates. Bests per car (by VIN, or the make, model and year in SETUP) go to `sdmc:/3ds/gauge-boy/bests.csv`. Plain text screens for now; the toy look comes in M5.
- **Freeze frames**: when SCAN finds stored codes, it also reads the freeze frame (Mode 02): the snapshot the engine computer kept when a code set (load, coolant, trims, MAP, RPM, speed, timing and more). DIAG's facts say which code it belongs to, and tapping that code on STORED shows "Freeze frame when it set: ..." on the top screen. It's saved in the code history too, as a `freeze` row.
- **REC on the TRIM screen**: records the fuel trims to a drive log like LIVE's REC: both banks' short- and long-term trims with a **Total** column per bank, plus RPM, speed, MAP or MAF and load for context, 5 rows a second, so LOG lists the recording and GRAPH replays it (e.g. trims at idle, then at 2500 rpm). TRIM also polls MAP / MAF and load slowly now, for that context. Drive logs from LIVE get the Total columns too when they hold both trims of a bank.
- **Vacuum on cars without a baro PID** (such as the Ridgeline and the Tracker): whenever Gauge Boy reads MAP with the engine off, it saves that reading for the car (by VIN, or by the make, model and year set in SETUP) in `sdmc:/3ds/gauge-boy/baro.csv`. The next LIVE session starts with it, so vacuum shows from the start and the drive log's Vacuum column is filled; LIVE marks it "saved baro" and the drive log header says when it was read. A new engine-off reading replaces it (read one again after driving somewhere much higher or lower).
- **GRAPH** on the LOG screen: plots one value of a recorded drive against time on the top screen, with axes, times and the shown part's min / max / average. Each pixel column keeps its lowest and highest value, so short spikes stay visible on long drives. **<** **>** pick another value, **ZOOM -** / **ZOOM +** show more or less of the drive, **<<** **>>** move earlier or later, **ALL** shows the whole drive. The file is read in the background, so the screen never waits on the SD card. A plain look for now; the toy style comes in M5.
- **LOG screen**: the LOG card on HOME now lists the drives recorded with REC (newest first, up to 100): date and time, car and length, three per page. Tap one to see its details on the top screen (date, car, VIN, protocol, length, size, units and the values recorded); **DELETE** removes it from the SD card after a confirmation. Files in `drives/` that aren't GaugeBoy drive logs are listed as such.
- **REC on the LIVE screen**: records the view's values to a CSV file on the SD card (`sdmc:/3ds/gaugeboy/drives/drive-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.csv`), 5 rows a second: time, each PID with its unit, battery voltage, and vacuum (negative under boost) when MAP is polled, under a header with the GaugeBoy version, date, car, VIN, protocol and units. The button turns red with the elapsed time, and the top screen shows `REC m:ss` and the file name. Start and stop it parked; it keeps recording while driving with no touch needed. REC again, STOP or BACK stops it; switching views starts a new file (the columns change); a lost connection pauses it in the same file; a full card or a 50 MB file stops it with a log line. Tapping REC while stopped starts polling too.

### Fixed

- **CHECK & CONNECT joins the adapter's Wi-Fi first**, as the old CONNECT did: it moves the 3DS off another network (such as home Wi-Fi) onto the chosen slot. Without it the 3DS stayed on home Wi-Fi and couldn't reach the adapter. With the slot on Any, a failure now names the network the 3DS is on. A stale connection is reconnected once before CHECK gives up.
- LOG > GRAPH left gaps wherever a value changed between neighbouring pixel columns; every step is joined now.
- LOG's list cut long car names short ("2008 Honda Ridgeli"); the row fits a 20-character car name now.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-16

M2 release: live data. **LIVE** shows the car's sensors in task-focused views (Engine, Idle & vacuum, Air & fuel, Warm-up, Drive, All, Custom) with how often each value refreshes, plus battery voltage and manifold vacuum or boost; **TRIM** shows short-term, long-term and total fuel trims for both banks with hints. Polling is fast: about 5.5 requests a second on ISO 9141-2 cars and about 14 a second on CAN cars, where one request carries several PIDs and only the engine computer is asked. GaugeBoy finds the car by itself after connecting and decodes the make and model from the VIN (NHTSA vPIC). SETUP is now four pages: TESTS, WI-FI, VEHICLE and OTHER (units, About). Tested on a 2008 Honda Ridgeline and a 1996 Geo Tracker (ISO 9141-2) and a 2023 Subaru Ascent (CAN, turbo).

### Added

- **The car is found on its own**: after GaugeBoy connects to the adapter, it finds the car's protocol and reads the VIN by itself (once per connection, when nothing else is running), so HOME, DIAG and SETUP > VEHICLE show the car, e.g. "2023 Subaru Ascent", before any SCAN. Codes show as "not read yet" until you SCAN. If the car doesn't answer (ignition off), it tries again every 20 s. A different car than the last scan clears the old codes. Cars without a VIN over OBD get the make, model and year set in SETUP > VEHICLE.
- **SETUP has four pages** (a menu of TESTS, WI-FI, VEHICLE, OTHER; BACK returns to the menu). **TESTS**: ATZ, ATSP0+0100, ATDPN, ATRV, 0902 VIN, SPEED TEST, LIVE TEST, SAVE LOG and the log's OLDER / NEWER. **WI-FI**: connect to the adapter, Wi-Fi slot, check the slot's IP and DNS, Wi-Fi setup guide. **VEHICLE**: make (the picker), model (typed on the 3DS keyboard), year (< > from 1996) and VIN (tap to read); values from the VIN win and are marked "(VIN)", your own are used for cars that don't report a VIN, and are saved as `model` and `year` under `[vehicle]`. **OTHER**: units (US or metric, saved as `units` under `[display]`, used by LIVE and the log) and About (version, license, data sources, safety notice).
- **LIVE TEST** on SETUP > TESTS: asks every supported PID once and logs each reply and decoded value, then polls the Engine view for 10 s and logs the requests per second, each PID's refresh rate and combined requests: one tap to check a car's decoding and speed.
- **Model from the VIN**: DIAG, code history and the adapter test's VIN read now show the make and model (for example "2023 Subaru Ascent"), decoded offline from NHTSA vPIC's VIN patterns (public domain) for model years 1996 and later. When the VIN has no pattern, the maker and year show as before.
- **Boost on turbo cars**: LIVE decodes PID 87 (wide-range intake manifold pressure, up to 2048 kPa), which cars such as the 2023 Subaru Ascent report instead of PID 0B. It counts as MAP for vacuum, and when manifold pressure is above barometric pressure the vacuum row becomes **Boost** in psi (kPa in metric). The Idle & vacuum and Air & fuel views now include PID 87 and barometric pressure (PID 33, read once a minute) where the car has them.
- **SPEED TEST** on the adapter test screen (in place of SETUP SLOT, which is in the SETUP list): times 10 of each kind of request (`ATRV` for the adapter alone, `010C`, `010C1` with a response count, and `010C1` with `ATAT2` and with `ATST19`) and logs min / average / max, to find where the time per request goes.
- LIVE polls by priority: each PID has a desired refresh time (RPM, speed, throttle, MAP and MAF as often as possible; short-term trims, load and timing twice a second; long-term trims every 5 s; intake air every 10 s; coolant and oil every 15 s; fuel level, baro and ambient every 30-60 s) and the most overdue one is read next, so on a slow bus the fast-changing values stay live. Slow PIDs show their refresh as "every 15 s".
- LIVE asks for one reply per request (`010C1`), so the adapter answers as soon as the car does instead of waiting for more ECUs; adapters that refuse it get plain requests. When LIVE stops, the log gets its requests per second and each PID's refresh rate.
- The build's git commit is shown after the version (`GaugeBoy 0.1.0+b4beb83`) in the header, the start-up screen and the log.
- On CAN cars, LIVE and TRIM ask for up to 6 PIDs in one request ("010C0D05..."): the most overdue PID plus the others that are due or read as often as possible. A car that doesn't answer combined requests gets one PID at a time automatically. Older buses (ISO 9141-2, J1850, KWP) always use one PID a request, as the standard requires. The LIVE summary in the log says which was used.
- DIAG shows a **TRIM** button when a lean or rich code (P0171, P0172, P0174, P0175) is stored or pending; it opens the TRIM screen, and BACK returns to DIAG.
- **TRIM screen**: the TRIM card on HOME shows short-term, long-term and total fuel trim for each bank (one bank on inline engines) as center-zero bars from -25 to +25 % in green (within 10 %), amber (10-20 %) or red (beyond), with an over-range mark, plus the engine speed and plain-language hints such as "Both banks lean at idle: possible vacuum leak" (hints, not diagnoses).
- **LIVE views**: LIVE opens a list of views, each a few PIDs for one job: Engine (RPM, speed, coolant, throttle, load), Idle & vacuum, Air & fuel, Warm-up (coolant and run time every second), Drive, plus All (every supported PID) and Custom (your own pick, with EDIT PIDS). All and Custom say how often the fast values refresh, since more PIDs refresh slower. < > step through the views; the last view is remembered (`view` under `[live]` in `config.ini`).
- **LIVE screen** (text dashboard; the toy-like gauges come in M3): the LIVE card on HOME polls the chosen Mode 01 PIDs the car supports and shows each value in US units with its refresh rate, plus the adapter's battery voltage and manifold vacuum (barometric pressure from PID 33, or MAP read with the engine off, minus MAP). Defaults: RPM, speed, coolant, intake air, throttle, load, MAP, timing, MAF. The bottom screen has a toggle for every supported PID (19 decoded, including fuel trims for both banks), START / STOP and BACK; the choice is saved as `pids` under `[live]` in `config.ini`. PIDs that keep answering NO DATA are dropped; a lost connection is reconnected while polling.

### Fixed

- On CAN cars with more than one computer answering (the Ascent's engine and transmission), combined LIVE requests lost the engine's later values: both computers' multi-part replies arrived mixed together and couldn't be told apart. While LIVE, TRIM and LIVE TEST poll a CAN car, GaugeBoy now asks the engine computer only (`ATSH 7E0`, or `DA10F1` on 29-bit CAN) and puts the all-computer address back when polling stops, so DIAG still hears every computer. Combined requests then carry the response count, so the adapter answers without waiting for its timeout. If the engine doesn't answer at that address, or the adapter refuses the count, GaugeBoy goes back to the previous way by itself. A single bad combined reply no longer turns combined requests off for the session. The LIVE summary in the log says "engine ECU only".
- On CAN cars, a PID left out of a combined reply counted as NO DATA, so three in a row could drop it from LIVE (seen a few times a minute on the Ascent). Now it is asked again on its own, and only NO DATA to that counts. The LIVE summary in the log says how many PIDs were left out and shows the first three replies, to find out why.
- Every adapter request took at least ~400 ms on a 3DS, even ones only the adapter answers. On the 3DS all of an app's socket calls go through one session with the network service, one at a time, and the connection-test thread kept it busy with 100 ms waits, so each of a request's socket calls queued behind it. No socket wait happens inside the service any more (zero-timeout checks with short sleeps between them), in the adapter connection and in the connection-test thread. Thanks to 3dsDL's notes for the explanation.
- After a reconnect whose adapter reset failed, the reset wasn't tried again, so the adapter's echo stayed on for the rest of the session. The reset is now only marked done when it worked, and a failed setup logs the command that failed.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-15

M1 release: GaugeBoy now reads the car. DIAG shows the check engine light, readiness monitors, stored / pending / permanent codes with plain-English descriptions (generic codes, plus manufacturer codes for 32 makes) and the VIN, and can clear codes behind a confirmation. Tested on both test cars over ISO 9141-2: a 2008 Honda Ridgeline (VIN read) and a 1996 Geo Tracker (no VIN over OBD, so the make is chosen in SETUP), with the Vgate iCar Pro Wi-Fi adapter (`V-LINK`). The LIVE, TRIM, FUN and LOG screens come in later versions.

### Added

- **New HOME screen**: drawn with graphics instead of text, with cream cards on a pale yellow background, color icons and the Nunito font (SIL OFL). Two status cards on top show the check engine light (amber when on; tap for DIAG) and whether the adapter is connected (tap for the Wi-Fi guide); six cards below, LIVE (Dash gauges), DIAG (Codes & CEL), TRIM (Fuel trims), FUN (Fun modes), LOG (Record drives) and SETUP (Settings), with a green outline on the last one tapped; a bar at the bottom shows START Close (tap it, or press START, to close GaugeBoy) or a short notice. METER is renamed LIVE.
- **Car make** in SETUP, for cars that don't report a VIN over OBD (such as the 1996 Geo Tracker test car): a picker with Auto and the 32 makes that have their own code meanings. The chosen make gives manufacturer codes their meanings, shows on DIAG as "Geo (set in SETUP)" and goes into the code history; a make read from the VIN always wins. Saved as `make` under `[vehicle]` in `config.ini`. The adapter test screen moved to an ADAPTER button next to BACK.
- **Wi-Fi setup guide** (SETUP > WI-FI GUIDE): five steps for saving the adapter's network with the manual IP and DNS GaugeBoy needs, with the values worked out from the adapter's address and shown the way the 3DS takes them (192.168.000.011). CHECK reports whether a slot is chosen and named, whether its IP and DNS match, whether the 3DS is on Wi-Fi and whether the adapter answers.
- **Code history**: every scan and every clear is appended to `sdmc:/3ds/gaugeboy/code-history.csv` (date, VIN, car, check engine light, type, code, description; one row per code, a "none" row for a clean scan), so codes are kept after they're cleared.
- **Clear codes** on the DIAG screen: CLEAR opens a question that explains it turns off the check engine light and resets the readiness monitors; only the red CLEAR CODES button clears (no button shortcut). GaugeBoy refuses if the car reports a speed above 0, then sends the clear and scans again, and reports when the car refuses (e.g. engine running).
- **HOME and DIAG screens** (the app now opens on HOME, a grid of six big buttons: LIVE, DIAG, TRIM, FUN, LOG, SETUP; DIAG and SETUP work in this version). DIAG: tabs STORED / PENDING / PERM / READY, a paged list of codes or readiness monitors (tap a row for its full description on the top screen), BACK, page < > and SCAN. SCAN connects to the car and reads the check engine light, readiness monitors, stored / pending / permanent codes (with descriptions for the car's make) and the VIN in one go; the top screen shows the facts (car, VIN, protocol, CEL, readiness, codes) and progress while scanning. Amber CEL lamp on the bottom screen. SETUP is a list like DIAG's: Wi-Fi slot (tap to change), check the slot's IP and DNS, save this session's log, and the adapter test screen, with BACK. B, X, L and R are shortcuts for BACK, SCAN and paging.
- The adapter test app's ATSP0+0100 button runs the full protocol search: after `0100` it reads the protocol (`ATDPN`) and the further supported-PID ranges (`0120`, `0140`, ...), and logs the protocol, how many ECUs answered and the list of supported PIDs for the compatibility list.
- The adapter test app's 0902 VIN button decodes the VIN for every protocol (multi-line ISO 9141-2 / J1850 / KWP replies and CAN) and logs it with the maker (from the first three characters) and model year, and warns if the check digit doesn't match.
- Plain-English descriptions for 9,342 generic trouble codes (P0, P2, P34-P3F, B0, B3, C0, C3, U0, U3), plus manufacturer-specific codes (P1, B1, C1, U1 and so on) for 32 makes, shown when the car's make is known. Other manufacturer codes show "Manufacturer-specific code". Descriptions come from [Wal33D/dtc-database](https://github.com/Wal33D/dtc-database) (MIT); see `CREDITS.md`.
- QR codes for installing with FBI (*Remote Install* > *Scan QR Code*): one at the top of the README that always installs the newest release, and one per release (`gaugeboy-qr.png`, also shown in the release notes) for that exact version. Made with `tools/make_qr.py`.
- Developer: ELM327 emulator scenarios for the non-CAN test cars (`ridgeline` and `tracker` on ISO 9141-2, `j1850` on J1850 VPW with codes set), now the default instead of the CAN `car`; `make emulator-check` runs a full DIAG scan on any protocol, and `tools/check-scenarios.sh` checks every scenario.

## [0.0.2] - 2026-11-15

Last M0 release: the 3DS now talks to a real Wi-Fi OBD-II adapter. Tested with a Vgate iCar Pro Wi-Fi adapter (ELM327 v2.3; its Wi-Fi network is called `V-LINK`) in a 2008 Honda Ridgeline. The 3DS normally drops a Wi-Fi network that has no internet, so GaugeBoy answers the 3DS's connection test itself. The adapter's Wi-Fi slot needs a manual IP and DNS; see `docs/testing-on-3ds.md` section 3.

### Added

- SETUP SLOT button on the adapter test screen: reads the chosen Wi-Fi slot's IP and DNS settings and, if they aren't what GaugeBoy needs, shows the current and needed values with where to enter them in System Settings. It does not change any settings: writing them from the app was tried and removed after it crashed the 3DS. The buttons are now in three columns.
- The adapter test app's start-up log shows each saved Wi-Fi slot's IP settings (automatic or manual IP, mask, gateway, DNS, MTU), read-only.
- SLOT button on the adapter test screen: choose which System Settings connection slot (any, 1, 2 or 3) CONNECT uses, so another saved network in range (such as home Wi-Fi) doesn't take over. Saved as `wifi_slot` in `config.ini`; the start-up log lists each slot's network name. Confirmed on a 3DS: it moves the 3DS from home Wi-Fi to the adapter's network in a few seconds.
- SAVE LOG button on the adapter test screen (between OLDER and NEWER): saves this session's log to `sdmc:/3ds/gaugeboy/logs/gaugeboy-log-<date>-<time>.txt` for sharing.
- GaugeBoy answers the 3DS's internet connection test itself: a background thread runs a tiny DNS server and the test page on the 3DS, so the 3DS keeps a Wi-Fi OBD-II adapter's network without a laptop or other device. The adapter's connection slot needs a manual IP with Primary DNS set to that same IP (see `docs/testing-on-3ds.md`). Confirmed on a 3DS with the Vgate iCar Pro adapter.
- `tools/fake_conntest.py`: a small DNS and web server that answers the 3DS's internet connection test, so a 3DS can stay on a Wi-Fi OBD-II adapter's network that has no internet. Dev tool for proving the fix; test steps in `tools/README.md`.
- CONNECT (and the automatic reconnect) first asks the 3DS to join a saved Wi-Fi connection if it isn't on one, the way Luma3DS's "Force wireless connection" does, to reach adapters that have no internet. The result, including the system's error codes on failure, is logged.
- Connection errors name the call that failed, for example `errno 115 at socket()`.
- The adapter test app logs a `Network:` line at start-up and before every connect attempt: whether the 3DS is on Wi-Fi, the network name and saved-connection slot, and the 3DS's own IP address (or "no IP address").

### Fixed

- GaugeBoy's connection-test web server answered every web request on the network (a file-transfer app polling the 3DS filled the log). It now serves the test page only to the 3DS's connection test, answers anything else with 404 and logs that once.
- CONNECT no longer asks the 3DS to join Wi-Fi when it is already on a network (the system's own join counts), which had made the 3DS rejoin and drop the adapter connection about 25 seconds later.
- Connecting to the adapter took the full 3-second timeout on the 3DS even when the adapter answered at once; a pending connect is now re-checked every 100 ms.
- "Wi-Fi state unknown (ac 0xE0A09D2E)" in the `Network:` line now reads "Wi-Fi on (joined by the system)" or "Wi-Fi not connected".
- On the 3DS, a TCP connect that was still in progress was reported as `errno 26: Text file busy` and abandoned. libctru returns the 3DS's raw socket error there (26 means "in progress"); GaugeBoy now translates it and keeps waiting until the connect timeout, then reports "Adapter did not answer".
- The `Network:` line numbered connection slots one too high, and "Wi-Fi state unknown" now includes the system's error code.
- Connection errors showed a negative errno with no description (for example `errno -115` when the 3DS had no network). They now show the positive value and its meaning (`errno 115: Network interface is not configured`).

## [0.0.1] - 2026-11-14

First test release of the M0 spike: an adapter test app for checking whether a New 3DS can talk to a Wi-Fi ELM327 adapter. It is not a finished diagnostics app yet.

### Added

- Project brief (`docs/gaugeboy-brief.md`) and agent instructions (`CLAUDE.md`).
- Beads issue tracking with milestone epics M0 to M7 and the M0 issue plan.
- README with the unofficial / not-affiliated statement and safety notice, MIT license, changelog and contributing guide.
- Project skeleton: `make` builds `gaugeboy.3dsx`, which enables New 3DS speedup mode, shows the startup safety notice, and shows an "unsupported device" screen and exits on Old 3DS models.
- Host unit tests: `make test` builds and runs `tests/` with the host C compiler using a small built-in assert harness.
- ELM327 driver (`source/elm327/`): sends commands, reads replies up to the `>` prompt with a timeout, cleans up echo, blank lines, `SEARCHING...`, garbage bytes and lower-case clone replies, and reports adapter errors (`?`, `NO DATA`, `UNABLE TO CONNECT`, `BUS INIT: ...ERROR`, `CAN ERROR`, `STOPPED` and others). Includes the `ATZ` / `ATE0` / `ATL0` / `ATSP0` start-up sequence and `ATRV` / `ATDPN` readers.
- TCP transport (`source/transport/`): connects to the adapter (default `192.168.0.10:35000`) with a connect timeout, reads and writes with timeouts so nothing blocks indefinitely, detects dropped connections and reconnects, backing off from 0.5 s up to 8 s while the adapter is unreachable. Starts the 3DS network service.
- M0 spike app: after the safety notice, the top screen shows a timestamped log and the bottom screen has touch buttons for CONNECT, ATZ, ATSP0+0100, ATDPN, ATRV and 0902 (VIN), plus log scrolling. Commands run on a worker thread; the log is also saved to `sdmc:/3ds/gaugeboy/spike-log.txt`.
- Settings file `sdmc:/3ds/gaugeboy/config.ini` with the adapter address (`[adapter]` `host` and `port`), created with defaults on first launch.
- `make cia` builds `gaugeboy.cia` (title ID `0004000003DEDF00`, product code `CTR-P-GBOY`) with an original gauge icon, banner and banner chime; the `.3dsx` uses the same icon. CI builds and uploads the `.cia` too.
- Guide for installing and testing on a real New 3DS (`docs/testing-on-3ds.md`).
- Release workflow: pushing a `v*` tag runs the tests, builds `gaugeboy.3dsx` and `gaugeboy.cia` and publishes them on a GitHub release under those fixed names, so the `releases/latest/download/` links always get the newest version. It stops if the tag, `source/version.h` and `CHANGELOG.md` disagree.
